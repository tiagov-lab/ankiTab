Shortcuts

  • Space / Enter: Show Answer, then Good
  • 1: Again · 2: Hard · 3: Good · 4: Easy (cards with fewer buttons: 1 Again · 2 Good · 3 Easy)
  • E: Edit note
  • R / F5: Replay audio
  • Ctrl+Z: Undo
  • @ (Shift+2): Suspend card
  • -: Bury card
